# Privacy Policy — Tidy Bookmarks

_Last updated: November 6, 2026_

Tidy Bookmarks does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- **Bookmarks.** The extension reads your bookmarks to find duplicates, empty folders and broken links.
  This happens entirely inside your browser. Your bookmarks are never sent to us or to anyone else.
- **Link checking.** Only when you press "Check links", the extension asks Chrome for permission to access
  websites and then requests each bookmarked page once to see whether it still exists. Requests are sent
  without your cookies. Only the result (works / gone / needs a look) is kept, in memory, and it is
  discarded when you close the page.
- **Backups.** Backup files are created on your computer and saved to your downloads folder. They are
  never uploaded.
- **No analytics, no ads, no accounts, no remote servers.**
